The Crowningჯვრისწერა

ჯვრისწერა

The Georgian Orthodox sacrament of marriage. There are no spoken vows; the service is sung in Georgian, and you only need to watch for five moments.

Gergeti sits at 2,170 m, so expect snow: wear a warm coat and boots for the walk up. Women cover their hair with a scarf. Guests stand during the service, about length TBC.
