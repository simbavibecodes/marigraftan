The Church Ceremonyჯვრისწერა

Before you comeსანამ მოხვალთ

Gergeti sits at 2,170 m, so expect snow: wear a warm coat and boots for the walk up. Women cover their hair with a scarf. Guests stand during the service, which lasts about 30 minutes.
